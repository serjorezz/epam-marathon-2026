# US5_Wine Category and Expanded Menu Catalog

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Epic** | Menu & Dish Management |
| **US name** | Wine Category and Expanded Menu Catalog |
| **Role** | Customer / Dining Guest |
| **Description** | As a customer or dining guest, I want to explore an expanded menu offering including a dedicated Wine category, additional appetizers, and desserts with complete nutritional and cultural descriptions, so that I can enjoy authentic Georgian wine pairings and broader dining choices. |

### Pre-conditions:
- The customer or guest is browsing the Menu page or ordering via pre-order on the Green & Tasty platform.
- The catalog includes the expanded menu items and category classifications.

---

### Acceptance Criteria (AC):

1. **Dedicated Wine Category Tab**:
   - The menu category navigation includes a dedicated *"Wine"* filter button alongside Appetizers, Main Courses, and Desserts.
   - Selecting the *"Wine"* tab filters the displayed catalog to show exclusively wine selections.

2. **Expanded Appetizers & Desserts Offerings**:
   - The Appetizers category includes the *"Georgian Pkhali Trio"* with complete descriptions and pricing.
   - The Desserts category includes the *"Honey Walnut Baklava"* with complete descriptions and pricing.

3. **Authentic Wine Selection**:
   - The Wine category presents at least three distinct regional Georgian wines:
     1. *"Saperavi Reserve Dry Red Wine"* (750ml, dry red wine from Kakheti).
     2. *"Rkatsiteli Qvevri Amber Wine"* (750ml, traditional UNESCO heritage amber wine).
     3. *"Kindzmarauli Semi-Sweet Red Wine"* (750ml, naturally semi-sweet red wine).
   - Each wine card displays its bottle volume (e.g., `750ml`), pricing, calorie count, and antioxidant notes.

4. **Multi-Language Content & Nutritional Data**:
   - Descriptions, ingredients, and nutritional specifications (calories, protein, fats, carbohydrates, and key vitamins/antioxidants) for all new menu items are fully translated in English, Ukrainian, and Georgian.
   - Weight and volume indicators reflect appropriate units (e.g., `750ml` for wines, `g` for food dishes) across all locales.

5. **Dish Details Modal Integration**:
   - Clicking any wine or new dish card opens the detailed modal displaying high-resolution imagery, full tasting notes, nutritional breakdown, and pre-order capabilities.

---

### Assumptions & Open Questions:

- **Assumptions**:
  - Wine selections can be added to pre-orders and group orders under the same ordering workflow as regular dishes.
  - Alcoholic beverage ordering conforms to local regulatory age requirements.
- **Open Questions**:
  - None.
